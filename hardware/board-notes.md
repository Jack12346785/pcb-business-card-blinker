# Board Notes

## Mechanical Direction

- Use a business-card-style rectangular PCB.
- Keep the final size within common cheap fab limits.
- Leave a clear area for name, handle, or contact details.

## Placement

- Put `BT1` on the back side to keep the front cleaner.
- Put `D1`, `D2`, `D3` on the front in a visible pattern.
- Put `SW1` on the front edge or lower corner for easy pressing.

## Routing

- Use a shared ground return to the battery negative terminal.
- Keep traces wide enough for easy manufacturing.
- Avoid unnecessary vias on the first revision.

## First Revision Goals

- Verify battery holder footprint
- Verify switch footprint
- Check LED polarity markings clearly on silkscreen
- Label all main parts on silkscreen

## Optional Improvements

- Add an on/off slide switch
- Add more LEDs if brightness and battery life remain acceptable
- Turn copper traces into part of the visual design
